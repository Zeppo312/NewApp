# Pregnancy guide covers

`body.jpg`, `heartburn.jpg` and `care.jpg` are the original generated editorial illustrations embedded in the accepted local concept `lottibaby-schwangerschaft-glaskacheln.html` (30 September 2026). Extracted without alteration on 3 October 2026. No BIÖG, NHS or stock source artwork is included.

Nine additional illustrations were generated on 3 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool, in the same warm gouache/pencil and muted pastel direction. The selected square originals were exported as JPEG at a maximum dimension of 1024 px (quality 84), without creative changes during export. They are saved here with versioned filenames. The partnership scene was edited to show a pregnant woman and her male partner; the previous variant is not bundled.

These are general scenes, not anatomical development images. No cover claims to represent a specific gestational age or medical finding. Images contain no text; labels are rendered accessibly in the app. All covers are bundled locally and have a static icon fallback.

The twelve-cover everyday series is now integrated. Six clinically reviewed anatomical stages and optional explanatory animations in package C remain separate production work. No anatomical draft is shipped as a reviewed image.

Generation prompts, selected source paths, the partnership correction and export evidence are recorded in [`output/pregnancy-images-20261003`](../../../output/pregnancy-images-20261003). The final prompt set is [`final-prompts.json`](../../../output/pregnancy-images-20261003/final-prompts.json).
