Lotti Baby supports you through pregnancy and everyday life with a baby. Record routines, save memories and organize your day in one place. The app is designed for parents and caregivers.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
Log sleep, breastfeeding, bottles and diaper changes. Use the sleep timer and review your previous entries. Add notes and keep track of your family's routines.

PREPARE FOR PREGNANCY AND BIRTH
Follow your pregnancy, collect questions for medical appointments and prepare your hospital bag. Record your birth preferences and plan upcoming appointments.

ORGANIZE TOGETHER
Connect your account with another caregiver to use shared information together. Plan tasks and appointments and keep shopping lists for family life.

SAVE MEMORIES
Record milestones with photos and notes. Log height and weight so you can find your own entries later.

IDEAS AND AI SUPPORT
Explore recipes and content for everyday life with a baby. Ask the AI assistant "Ask Lotti" questions. AI answers can contain mistakes.

ACCOUNT AND SUBSCRIPTION
You need an account for personal use. Access and available features depend on your entitlement or subscription. Available plans, prices and billing periods are shown before purchase in the app and on Google Play.

YOUR DATA
Account information and saved family and tracking data are processed through a backend. Depending on the features you use, other services may be involved, including AI, weather and notifications. See the privacy policy for details. You can request account deletion in the app.

HEALTH NOTICE
Lotti Baby is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. The app is intended for record keeping and general guidance. Content, summaries and AI answers do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a physician or another qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
