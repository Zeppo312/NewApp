Lotti Baby: app data and cloud storage

Lotti Baby is for parents and caregivers. Data is stored both on your device and in the cloud. Accounts, synchronization across devices and sharing use Supabase. Uninstalling the app therefore does not remove your cloud data.

Depending on the features used, we process email address, user ID, login information, profile name and image; baby name and birth date; pregnancy and due-date information; sleep, breastfeeding, meals, diapers, weight, height, development and custom activities; appointments, tasks, shopping lists, diary entries, notes, photos, community posts, messages and voice messages. This can include your or your child's health data. Permissions to share, settings, device and connection data and subscription information are also processed.

Local storage includes the login session, settings and cached content. Linked partners and invited caregivers can access shared family content according to their permissions. Community content is visible to its audience; publicly hosted images may be accessible through their URL.

Supabase – accounts, database and files

We use Supabase (Supabase Inc.) for authentication, account management, database and file storage, synchronization and server functions. The account, family, tracking and communication data described above and uploaded files are sent to and stored with Supabase. Technical requests also include connection data such as IP address, timestamps and authentication information.

This supports the features you use and access from other devices. Network connections use HTTPS/TLS and access is controlled through account and sharing permissions. This is not a promise of end-to-end encryption: server functions process content for synchronization, notifications and AI requests.

Necessary account and service processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; abuse prevention on Art. 6(1)(f). Additional requirements for health data are explained below. Provider information: https://supabase.com/privacy and https://supabase.com/dpa.

OpenAI – Ask Lotti, Care and voice input

AI features use the OpenAI API through Supabase server functions. Ask Lotti sends your question, the included conversation history and selected tracking summaries or age and pregnancy information relevant to the question. Free text can contain names and health information.

Care can send the baby's first name and age, selected sleep, feeding, diaper and weather facts, or the parent's first name, pregnancy week, trimester and days until the due date to personalize messages. New Care requests start only when you open Care in the active app. There is no automatic daily analysis or automatically generated Care push notification. Moving the app to the background aborts the running request on your device; content already sent to the server or OpenAI may still finish processing there. The at-my-limit selection is stored in the account but is not itself sent to OpenAI.

Voice input sends your recording for transcription and the resulting text for entry suggestions. Context may include baby name, device time, language, recent breast/bottle preference and custom activity names. Text you choose to turn into tasks, appointments or shopping items may also be processed. Confirmed entries and Care messages are stored in Supabase.

OpenAI states that API content is not used for training by default. Text-request abuse logs may normally remain for up to 30 days, longer if legally required; the transcription endpoint lists no application or abuse-log retention. This does not promise immediate deletion of all AI data. Details: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/ and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data.

Purposes are answering questions, wording Care messages and processing input. Requested services rely on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where consent is required, particularly for health data, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) apply. You can avoid AI and voice requests and opening AI-assisted Care. You may also withdraw consent for the future through support@lottibaby.de.

Sentry – diagnostics

The app initializes Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) at startup to detect and fix errors. Data may include crash reports, error messages and stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device information, timestamps, technical identifiers and preceding interaction and network events. Subscription-check failures additionally include a shortened, hashed user ID. This is pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Session recordings are disabled in this app version. The replay integration is removed and both recording rates are set to zero. Default PII enrichment is also disabled. This does not fully exclude personal information in errors, URLs, logs or voluntary feedback; transmissions also involve connection data.

The configured Sentry endpoint is in the USA. Necessary diagnostics for stability rely on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Diagnostic data and any recordings previously stored by older app versions are retained according to their purpose and the Sentry project's retention settings. Disabling recordings does not automatically delete older data. Contact support@lottibaby.de for access or deletion. Provider information: https://sentry.io/privacy/ and https://sentry.io/legal/dpa/.

RevenueCat, Apple and Google – purchases and subscriptions

RevenueCat, Inc. verifies, restores and associates subscriptions with your account. Your Supabase user ID is used as the RevenueCat App User ID. Data includes this ID, purchase receipts or Google Play purchase tokens, product and transaction IDs, plan, purchase and expiry dates, renewal and refund status, and technical app and device information. Access checks may contact RevenueCat even when you are not making a purchase.

RevenueCat sends subscription events to our Supabase backend, where account-linked status and entitlements unlock purchased features. Tracking entries, baby photos and AI questions are not part of the data we designate for subscription checks.

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments under its own terms. We do not receive full card or bank details through these purchases. RevenueCat also processes data in the USA. Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for subscriptions, Art. 6(1)(f) for abuse prevention and Art. 6(1)(c) for required statutory retention. Provider information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa; https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Other feature-specific transfers and permissions

Push notifications use Expo and Apple's or Google's delivery services, processing push tokens, delivery information and, depending on the notification, titles, previews and related IDs. Previews may contain message or family content and appear on your lock screen. Manage notifications in the app and device settings.

Baby Weather sends coordinates when you use device location, or a place you enter, to OpenWeather. Care weather uses Open-Meteo with coordinates rounded to approximately one kilometre. This rounding does not apply to all weather requests. Direct requests also disclose your IP address to the provider. You can revoke location permission in device settings.

Camera, photo-library and microphone access support the recording or upload feature you choose. Shared photos and voice messages can be stored in Supabase; AI voice input also follows the OpenAI section. Embedded recipe videos, thumbnails and external images disclose technical connection data to YouTube/Google or Unsplash. Barcode product searches send the code to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts or Open Products Facts.

Health data, consent and international processing

Pregnancy, development, nutrition and health information may be special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR. Processing requires both an Art. 6 legal basis and an exception under Art. 9(2), particularly explicit consent. Accepting terms, granting a device permission or reading this policy does not replace required separate consent. Only provide information about your child or others where you are authorized to do so.

Cloud providers may process data outside the EU/EEA, particularly in the USA. We do not promise exclusively German or European storage. Transfers require an applicable adequacy decision or safeguards under Art. 46 GDPR, particularly EU Standard Contractual Clauses. DPF certification is relevant only to the particular certified recipient and its scope. Request information and a copy of the safeguards for your data through support@lottibaby.de.

Retaining, exporting and deleting app data

Account and saved app content is retained to provide your account and chosen features until you delete content or request deletion of the account and data. Disabling a feature does not automatically delete existing entries. Technical logs are retained as needed for troubleshooting and security; subscription data for access checks, restoring purchases and resolving claims. Statutory retention of business records does not justify retaining all baby or health data.

Delete your account from Profile or delete all data and optionally your account in App Settings under Data & privacy. Manage account & data provides account deletion even without an active subscription and also offers export. Account deletion covers associated personal app data, subject to purpose-limited legal exceptions.

Outside the app, request deletion of your Lotti Baby account and associated data by emailing support@lottibaby.de with the subject “Lotti Baby – delete account”. Include your account email address, never your password. We may verify account ownership to prevent unauthorized deletion. You can also request access, correction, portability, restriction or deletion involving our providers through this contact.

In-app deletion does not necessarily immediately erase all data already processed by OpenAI, Sentry or RevenueCat. Their retention and deletion procedures apply; backups may expire on a later cycle. We handle your request including the affected processors and explain any necessary continued retention and its reason.

Deleting the account or uninstalling does not automatically cancel a store subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Apple App Store or Google Play. The withdrawal, objection and complaint rights in this policy also apply.

Google – optional sign-in with Google

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, you are redirected to Google to authenticate. Google confirms your identity to Supabase; we receive your Google account identifier, email address and profile information such as your name and, where available, profile picture. We do not receive your Google password and do not request access to your Google files, contacts or calendars.

This sign-in provides access to your Lotti Baby account. Supabase may associate your Google identity with an existing account using the same verified email address. A different address, such as a private Apple relay address, is not automatically merged with your existing account. The offered email sign-in remains available.

Google also processes technical connection data during sign-in. Provider information: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Supabase stores authentication data as part of account management; the information about Supabase and account deletion also applies to this sign-in.
